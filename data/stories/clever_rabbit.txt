Once upon a time, in a dense forest, there lived a fierce lion who declared himself the king of all animals. Every day, he would demand a different animal be sent to him for his meal.

Terrified, the animals agreed to take turns. One day, it was a rabbit’s turn. But the rabbit was wise. He walked slowly and arrived late.

The lion roared in anger. “Why are you late?” he growled.

The rabbit replied, “Oh mighty king, another lion stopped me and claimed he was the real king!”

Enraged, the lion asked to be taken to this rival. The rabbit led him to a deep well.

The lion looked into the well, saw his own reflection, and jumped in to attack — never to return.

From that day, the animals lived peacefully in the forest.
